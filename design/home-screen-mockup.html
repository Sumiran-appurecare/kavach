Nyaya Kavach · home screen · v2 · one screen, no scroll

Every feature on one screen, and only one of them is red.

A no-scroll home is a budget: 812 pixels, and nothing earns space twice. So the eleven things this product actually does each appear exactly once, the green panel carries identity and status, and the emergency path keeps the only red on the screen — because most days this is a filing cabinet, and roughly once a year it is the screen someone opens twenty minutes after ₹84,000 left their account.

State
NO SCROLL

The grid absorbs the slack, so nothing ever clips

The screen is a flex column at a fixed 812px. Panel, emergency bar, alerts and nav take the height they need; the feature grid takes whatever is left, with rows set to minmax(56px, 1fr). When a state adds height — an open case grows the panel — the grid gives the pixels back instead of pushing a scrollbar into existence.

DEDUPED

Your reference showed three features twice

"My Documents" and "Legal Vault" are one module. "Scam Check" and "Cyber Shield" are one module. "AI Assistant" sat in both rows. On a screen with no scroll that is roughly 120 wasted pixels, and it makes a user wonder whether two tiles do two different things. Every feature here appears once — Vault, Understand, Ask, Scam Check, Advocate, Family, Plan, Guides, plus Emergency on its own bar.

Emergency is a bar, not a tile in a row of five

In your reference it was a 70px square between Scam Check and My Documents. A person whose hands are shaking should not have to read five labels. It is full width, it is the only thing on the screen wearing this red, and no other feature may borrow the colour — the moment it appears twice it stops meaning emergency.

"We never ask for your OTP or PIN" is welded to the red bar

Not a footer line. The moment a user taps for help is the exact moment a real fraudster calls claiming to be us, so the notice travels with the button. It repeats on every assistant and emergency screen.

An open case replaces the marketing, not the features

Switch to Fraud case open. The status line and location pill give way to a live RBI window countdown with both reports shown separately — bank filed, 1930 still pending. One report is worth very little; zero liability needs both inside five calendar days, so the missing one is the loudest thing in the panel.

The location pill has a job

It is labelled advocate jurisdiction rather than being a decorative address bar. City is what allocation filters on — district first, then language, then availability — so the user can see and correct the thing that decides who picks up their case.

Every tile carries live state instead of a description

"24 documents", "2 left this year", "3 checks left this month". Switch to Free tier and the numbers change, not the layout. Counts come from the server's entitlement record — the client hides UI, the API decides what is allowed.

FR-EMG-03

Free still gets the red bar

Free has no assistant line, so the copy stops promising a callback and offers what costs us nothing: both numbers and the words to say, immediately. Metering someone at 2am who is losing money is the wrong trade, and it is the honest pitch — what Shield sells is someone staying on the line.

FR-CMP-04

Information and advice never look alike

"Ask a Question" is permanently labelled information, not advice. "Talk to an Advocate" carries the seal motif and promises a letterhead opinion. Two visual worlds, so nobody mistakes a chatbot answer for a lawyer's.

COLOUR

Forest green, ivory, brass, one red

Green from your reference, but pushed deeper (#123D2F) so white type clears AA on it. Brass (#8C6510) carries every deadline, green every healthy state, red only the emergency. Four jobs, four colours — a user learns the code in one session. Both light and dark are drawn; try your system setting.

Deliberately absent

No advocate list, photo or star rating, and no advocate named anywhere a prospective user can see. No price and no "Upgrade ₹999" button — the plan tile links to web checkout, because a Play Store purchase button hands 15–30% of a ₹999 product to the store. And no promise of recovering money, in either language: the offer is filing and follow-up.
